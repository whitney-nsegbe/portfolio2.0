Featured Project

uFindIt

My friends and I built uFindIt after noticing how often students lose things in classrooms or study spaces but never think to check the official lost & found. Most items aren’t really stolen, sometimes they’re just in the wrong place.

The platform lets students post items they lost or found and reconnect directly in a structured way (instead of posting on reddit). It supports real users, guest browsing, verification emails, and claim validation to keep interactions safe.

I worked primarily on the backend logic and authentication flows, helping design how users, guests, and item claims are handled in a live environment. We also reached out to university staff to make sure the platform complements official services rather than replacing them.
